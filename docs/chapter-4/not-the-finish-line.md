# Modernization is not the finish line

![Slide: Modernization is not the finish line, it is the launchpad for innovation. Migrate, Modernize and Transform.](../assets/34-not-the-finish-line.png){ .slide loading=lazy }

From migration to innovation: your apps, your data, your AI, built on Azure.

## Details

- **Migrate:** move apps and data to Azure, securely and at scale.
- **Modernize:** upgrade to modern platforms and APIs, ready for AI.
- **Transform:** build AI agents that deliver real business outcomes.
