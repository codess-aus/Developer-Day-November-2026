# Simpler access to AI across your development organisation

![Slide: GitHub Enterprise, generally available. Includes access to Copilot. A billing and licensing screen shows Copilot alongside Enterprise Cloud and Advanced Security.](../assets/3-simpler-access-to-ai-across-your-development-organization.png){ .slide loading=lazy }

**GitHub Enterprise** (generally available) includes access to Copilot for customers who choose this offering. That helps reduce barriers to AI adoption across the organisation.

**Takeaway:** bring AI into the workflows your team already uses, across Plan, Build, Review and Ship.
